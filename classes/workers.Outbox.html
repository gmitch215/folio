    Class Outbox

    A durable outbox for effects that must not be lost or repeated without notice: mail, outbound requests, Redis commands, cron units. Rows live in the task table of the object's own SQLite.

    Each row goes through four steps in this order: claim, flush, effect, complete, flush. The flush after the claim is what gives report its meaning. A reset after it leaves a running row that a successor knows may have run its effect, and the successor ends it as failed instead of running it again. A reset before it loses the claim, and nothing ran. The flush after the completion keeps a finished effect from being replayed after a reset.

    So a resend kind runs its effect at least once and a report kind at most once. A reported row is the operator's: it is final, and sending the effect again means a new id.
